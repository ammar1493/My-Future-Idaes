# SANAD — Fire Fighting Team Leader (3-Day Course)

A complete PowerPoint training course built from the NEFT Energies technical proposal
**NERFQ26-5018 — Fire Fighting Team Leader Training**, tailored for SANAD drilling
and workover rig crews operating for Saudi Aramco in Saudi Arabia.

## Course structure

- **Day 1 — LEAD: The Leader Under Pressure** (Modules 1–2)
  Leadership in emergency response, command presence, OODA decision-making,
  the first-60-seconds drill, stress/human factors, panic management, discipline.
- **Day 2 — COMMAND: Tactics, Coordination, Control** (Modules 3–5)
  Rig-specific fire hazards, fire behavior, scene size-up, dynamic risk assessment,
  attack strategies, radio discipline and CAN SITREPs, incident command, control
  zones, escalation tiers (rig team → SANAD ERT → Saudi Aramco Fire Protection →
  Civil Defense), accountability and PAR.
- **Day 3 — PROVE: Full-Scale Simulation & Evaluation** (Module 6)
  Two full-scale multi-risk simulation rounds, real-time leadership evaluation,
  structured debrief, final assessment (40/40/20), competency validation and
  certification, plus a 30-day back-on-the-rig action plan.

Beyond the six proposal modules, the expanded deck adds: course housekeeping and
ground rules, the Fire Team Leader competency profile (knows/does/is), a standards
& alignment slide, fire-team role anatomy, authority & duty of care, the Piper Alpha
leadership case study, stress inoculation, everyday leadership, a dedicated H2S
slide, pre-incident planning, water & foam logistics, the brief-back order standard,
alarm levels & ERP integration, muster procedure, MAYDAY/firefighter-down response,
exercise safety rules, detailed briefs for both simulation rounds, feedback skills,
a sample knowledge check, and an annual drill-rhythm calendar.

Each day includes a timed schedule slide; 60–65% of the course is practical,
matching the NEFT proposal. **Every slide carries full instructor speaker notes** —
delivery guidance, timings, facilitation drills and discussion questions.
